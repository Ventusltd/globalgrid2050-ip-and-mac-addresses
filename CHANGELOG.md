## 2026-09-25 — first identity registry

UUID4 issuance, numeric identity encoding, event-replayed records, stable web resolver,
top search, QR labels and explicit retirement. Three demonstration identities.
Independent ZXing decoding checks exported QR geometry at two sizes.
Registry regression tests cover immutable fields, duplicates, hostile addresses,
rewritten history and failed writes. No live hardware/network allocation or
billion-record hosting is claimed. Publication uses one unscheduled workflow.
